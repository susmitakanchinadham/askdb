Slice 3: Executor, authorizer, limits and pagination

Added
- src/askdb/authorizer.py: make_authorizer(tables, columns). Allows SELECT, reads of exactly the
  validator's tables/columns (empty column name allowed for COUNT(*)), and a function allowlist.
  Denies every other action (writes, PRAGMA, ATTACH, recursion, extensions, unknown functions).
- src/askdb/executor.py: Executor(db_path, timeout=10, max_concurrent=4, row_ceiling=1000).
  execute(validated, page) opens a fresh open_readonly connection, installs the authorizer built
  from result.tables/columns and a progress-handler deadline, runs result.sql only.
  Pages of 25 via LIMIT/OFFSET; ordered_sql() appends every result column by position to the
  ORDER BY as a tie-breaker; total is counted up to ceiling+1 so truncation is flagged.
  Errors: QueryTimeout, TooBusy (non-blocking semaphore), ExecutionError.
- Tests: tests/integration/test_authorizer.py, tests/integration/test_executor.py,
  tests/unit/test_pagination.py.

Verified
- Full suite: 238 passed. ruff check and format clean.
- All golden SQL returns rows under the authorizer; runaway 4-way join is interrupted at 0.3 s;
  1,831-row query truncates at 1,000 and is flagged; pages 1 and 2 are disjoint and stable even
  when ordering on a low-cardinality column; concurrency cap refuses a second query and releases.
